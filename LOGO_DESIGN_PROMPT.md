# 🎨 PROMPT THIẾT KẾ LOGO CHO ỨNG DỤNG FOOD DELIVERY

## Prompt chính cho AI Art Generator (DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion):

```
Design a modern, minimalist logo for a food delivery mobile app. 
The logo should feature:
- A stylized shopping bag or delivery bag icon
- Food elements like a bowl, plate, or utensils subtly integrated
- Clean, rounded shapes with smooth curves
- Vibrant gradient colors: orange (#FF6B35) to red (#FF1744) or warm tones
- White or light colored icon on transparent background
- Modern, friendly, and appetizing appearance
- Suitable for mobile app icon (square format, 512x512px recommended)
- Flat design style with subtle shadows or depth
- Professional yet approachable aesthetic
- Vietnamese food delivery app branding
```

## Prompt chi tiết hơn (Premium):

```
Create a professional logo design for a Vietnamese food delivery mobile application called "Codedoan". 

Design Requirements:
- Style: Modern flat design with subtle 3D depth
- Main Element: A stylized delivery bag or shopping bag
- Secondary Elements: Food bowl, steam lines, or utensils
- Color Palette: 
  * Primary: Vibrant Orange (#FF6B35)
  * Secondary: Warm Red (#FF1744) 
  * Accent: Golden Yellow (#F7931E)
  * Background: White or transparent
- Shape: Rounded square or circular badge style
- Mood: Friendly, fast, reliable, appetizing
- Typography: If including text, use modern sans-serif, bold
- Icon Style: Clean lines, rounded corners, minimal details
- Use Case: Mobile app icon, splash screen, branding
- Dimensions: 512x512 pixels, scalable vector
- Background: Transparent PNG or white background
- Avoid: Complex details, too many colors, cluttered design
```

## Prompt cho DALL-E 3:

```
A modern, minimalist logo for a food delivery mobile app. The design features a stylized white shopping bag icon with a food bowl visible inside. The background uses a vibrant gradient from orange (#FF6B35) to red (#FF1744). The style is flat design with rounded corners, clean lines, and a professional appearance. The logo should be suitable for use as a mobile app icon. Square format, transparent background, high resolution.
```

## Mô tả chi tiết cho Designer:

### Concept:
Logo cho ứng dụng giao đồ ăn nhanh, hiện đại, thân thiện với người dùng Việt Nam.

### Elements:
1. **Bag Icon**: Túi đựng đồ ăn cách điệu, bo tròn góc
2. **Food Symbol**: Bát/tô đồ ăn hoặc món ăn nhỏ bên trong túi
3. **Steam Lines**: Vài đường hơi nước nhẹ nhàng (optional)
4. **Handle**: Tay cầm túi mềm mại, bo tròn

### Colors:
- **Primary**: #FF6B35 (Cam đậm)
- **Secondary**: #FF1744 (Đỏ)
- **Accent**: #F7931E (Vàng cam)
- **Icon**: #FFFFFF (Trắng)
- **Background**: Transparent hoặc gradient

### Style Guidelines:
- Flat design với depth nhẹ
- Rounded corners (border radius lớn)
- Clean, minimal
- Friendly và approachable
- Professional nhưng không quá nghiêm túc
- Dễ nhận biết ở kích thước nhỏ

### Technical Specs:
- Format: SVG (vector) hoặc PNG 512x512px
- Background: Transparent
- Scalable: Hoạt động tốt từ 16px đến 512px
- Export: @1x, @2x, @3x cho iOS/Android

- Test logo ở nhiều kích thước khác nhau
- Đảm bảo contrast tốt với background
- Giữ design đơn giản, dễ nhận biết
